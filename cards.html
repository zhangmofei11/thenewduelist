Deck Building Guide

Deck Composition

  • • 20-24 Cards total in your deck
  • • 8-10 Attack Cards for direct damage
  • • 6-8 Defense Cards for protection
  • • 4-6 Spell Cards for utility and control

Pro Tips

  • 1. Focus on 1-2 element types for synergy bonuses
  • 2. Balance high-cost and low-cost cards for tempo
  • 3. Include 2-3 defensive cards for sustainability
  • 4. Test your deck in practice mode before ranked
