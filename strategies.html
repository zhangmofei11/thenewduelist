Strategy Guides

🔥

Fire Aggro Strategy

Overwhelm opponents with rapid attacks before they can set up defenses. Focus on low-cost, high-damage cards.

Key Cards: Inferno Dragon, Flame Sprite

Win Condition: Deal 8000+ damage by turn 6

💧

Water Control Strategy

Deny opponent resources while building up powerful late-game threats. Patience is key.

Key Cards: Tidal Guardian, Frost Elemental

Win Condition: Outlast opponent's resources

🌍

Earth Ramp Strategy

Accelerate mana growth to play powerful cards earlier than opponents expect.

Key Cards: Stone Golem, Earthshaker

Win Condition: Cheat out high-cost cards

💨

Wind Tempo Strategy

Strike fast and evade counterattacks. Keep pressure on while maintaining board control.

Key Cards: Storm Phantom, Zephyr Blade

Win Condition: Chain attacks for multiplier damage

✨

Arcane Balance Strategy

Adapt to any situation with flexible card choices. Play reactively and punish mistakes.

Key Cards: Arcane Sovereign, Mana Crystal

Win Condition: Answer every threat efficiently

⚔️

Mirror Match Tips

When facing the same deck type, focus on resource management and baiting key cooldowns.

Key Tip: Don't blow your finisher too early

Key Tip: Track opponent's hand size
