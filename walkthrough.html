Campaign Walkthrough

Complete guide to conquering all 5 realms and defeating every boss

Story Overview

You are a budding duelist in the realm of Aetheria, where magical cards are the source of all power. When the corrupt Arcane Council seizes control, you must journey across five distinct realms, mastering the art of card combat, forging alliances, and ultimately challenging the Council to restore peace to the land.

Chapter Guide

Chapter 1

The Spark of Battle

Ember Peaks

Your journey begins in the humble village of Hearth. Learn the basics of combat against local rivals and discover your affinity for Fire magic.

Recommended Level: 1-3 | Battles: 3 | Boss: Ember Scout Captain

Boss Strategy - Ember Scout Captain

Uses basic Fire attacks. Keep your defense up and attack during his cooldowns. He has 1500 HP.

Chapter 2

Trial by Fire

Ember Peaks

Venture deeper into volcanic terrain. Face tougher opponents and prove your worth at the Ember Arena tournament.

Recommended Level: 4-6 | Battles: 5 | Boss: Pyromancer Vex

Boss Strategy - Pyromancer Vex

Spells deal burn damage over time. Bring healing cards and don't let your HP drop below 50% for too long. He has 3000 HP.

Chapter 3

Into the Depths

Azure Depths

Journey to the underwater kingdom of Pelagios. The environment changes combat with water-based puzzles and currents.

Recommended Level: 7-9 | Battles: 4 | Boss: Leviathan Knight

Boss Strategy - Leviathan Knight

High defense, low attack. Chip away with water spells and save your ultimate for when he's stunned. He has 4000 HP.

Chapter 4

The Coral Throne

Azure Depths

Discover the truth about the sunken kingdom's alliance with the Arcane Council. Navigate political intrigue while dueling for survival.

Recommended Level: 10-12 | Battles: 6 | Boss: Queen Tidalis

Boss Strategy - Queen Tidalis

Summons water minions. Clear them quickly or they'll overwhelm you. Focus on the Queen when she appears. She has 5000 HP.

Chapter 5

Ancient Awakening

Stone Giants

Enter the territory of the Stone Giants, ancient beings who have watched over the realm for millennia.

Recommended Level: 13-15 | Battles: 5 | Boss: Granite Guardian

Boss Strategy - Granite Guardian

Extremely high defense but slow. Use Earth-type cards for bonus damage. Patience wins this fight. He has 6000 HP.

Chapter 6

The Titan's Challenge

Stone Giants

Face the greatest of the Stone Giants in an epic colosseum battle witnessed by hundreds of giants.

Recommended Level: 16-18 | Battles: 4 | Boss: Elder Titan Moloch

Boss Strategy - Elder Titan Moloch

Enrages at 50% HP, doubling his attack but halving his defense. Save your burst damage for this phase. He has 7000 HP.

Chapter 7

Skies of Fury

Sky Citadel

Ascend to the floating Sky Citadel. Wind currents affect combat positioning and spell trajectories.

Recommended Level: 19-21 | Battles: 5 | Boss: Storm Warden Zephyr

Boss Strategy - Storm Warden Zephyr

Fast and evasive. Use Wind-type cards to match his speed, or lock him down with Earth stun effects. He has 5500 HP.

Chapter 8

The Cloud King

Sky Citadel

Challenge the ruler of the Sky Citadel for the secret passage to the Arcane Core.

Recommended Level: 22-24 | Battles: 6 | Boss: Cloud King Altius

Boss Strategy - Cloud King Altius

Has two phases. In second phase, he summons wind clones. Kill clones first or they'll heal him. He has 8000 HP total.

Chapter 9

Heart of Magic

Arcane Core

Enter the source of all magical power. Reality bends and warps as you approach the Council's seat of power.

Recommended Level: 25-27 | Battles: 7 | Boss: Arcane Sentinel

Boss Strategy - Arcane Sentinel

Uses random element types each turn. Adapt quickly and don't rely on elemental synergies. He has 6500 HP.

Chapter 10

Final Duel

Arcane Core

Face the ultimate challenge: the corrupt Arcane Council itself. Only the worthy can restore balance to Aetheria.

Recommended Level: 28-30 | Battles: 3 | Boss: The Arcane Council (Final)

Final Boss Strategy - The Arcane Council

Three-phase battle against Council members:

  • Phase 1: Fire Councilor (4000 HP) - Aggressive
  • Phase 2: Water Councilor (4500 HP) - Control
  • Phase 3: Arcane Overlord (5000 HP) - All elements

Bring a balanced deck with answers for each element. Save your best cards for Phase 3.

General Tips

Before Boss Battles

  • • Fully upgrade your deck
  • • Complete side quests for bonus items
  • • Review boss patterns in advance
  • • Save before attempting

Resource Farming

  • • Daily quests give bonus XP
  • • Replay Chapter 2 for gold farming
  • • Arena gives card packs
  • • Trade duplicates with players

Leveling Fast

  • • Focus main story for XP
  • • Use XP boost items
  • • Don't skip dialogue (bonus XP)
  • • Unlock hidden areas for bonus
