Getting Started

1

Choose Your Starter Deck

Select from Fire, Water, Earth, or Wind starter decks. Each has unique strengths and playstyles.

2

Learn the Basics

Understand mana management, card types (Attack, Defense, Spell), and turn structure.

3

Complete the Tutorial

Play through the first 3 chapters to unlock all core game features and earn rewards.

4

Build Your Collection

Earn cards through battles, daily quests, and the in-game shop to refine your strategy.
